How the price adjustment works

Adjusted figures restate each historical dollar in the dollars of the year you pick, using the Producer Price Index for Architectural, Engineering, and Related Services (BLS series PCU54135413, not seasonally adjusted), annual averages. This tracks the price of architectural and engineering services specifically, which is more defensible for fee comparisons than a consumer price index.

adjusted = original × (PPI of selected year ÷ PPI of the phase's rate-table year)

Because it's a ratio of two endpoints, every year in between is already baked into those index levels — nothing is missed, even across a long gap.

To get the latest index value, visit FRED PCU54135413 and read the most recent annual average from the data table.
